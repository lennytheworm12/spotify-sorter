❓ FAQ

The clip sounds like a podcast / spoken word / weird field recording.
That's expected — the library includes experimental material, and these clips are here to test how the AI behaves where music structure is missing. Quick guide for speech clips:

  • Melody → rate X — speech has no musical tune to compare
  • Rhythm → rate X unless the speaking cadence match is genuinely striking (don't strain — cadence isn't what the AI measures)
  • Timbre → rateable! Voice texture and recording character count (two grainy radio voices are timbre-similar)

X ratings are excluded from statistics but count as done — an honest X from you is worth far more than a guessed number.

Should I judge genre, lyrics, or whether I like it?
No — only the named factor on the card.

Can I change a rating?
Yes — tap another button; if it was your rating it updates, others' ratings are kept.

Someone else rated the same card.
Great — every opinion is kept and shown as "· N ratings". Your tap adds yours.

Progress saved?
Instantly, always. Close the tab whenever you like — come back later, enter your name, and continue where you stopped.

Too quiet / too loud?
Use the 🔊 slider (up to 200%). Above ~150% distortion may affect your judgment.

Why does this exist?
We're measuring whether an AI music-similarity system agrees with human hearing. Your ratings are the human half of that comparison.
